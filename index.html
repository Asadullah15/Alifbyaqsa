Our Story

Meet the Kitchen Behind Alif by Aqsa

Hello, I am Aqsa. I’ve been cooking since my early teens, and somewhere along the way, the kitchen became my favourite place to experiment, create, and, of course, eat! As a food content creator, I love sharing the flavours I grew up loving, especially the rich, comforting goodness of Hyderabadi food. From classic recipes to my own little twists, there’s always something cooking. I have a particular soft spot for anything meaty—slow-cooked, spicy, juicy, messy… you name it! But I also love trying new flavours, playing around with ingredients, and seeing where a little kitchen curiosity takes me. This website is where I’m bringing all of that together. So, whether you’re craving a proper Hyderabadi meal, looking for something new to try, or just here because food makes you happy, have a look around. Pick a recipe, get into the kitchen, and make it your own. Let’s cook, experiment, and eat something delicious!
